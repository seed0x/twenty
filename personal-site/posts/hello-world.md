Every developer has written a "hello world" post. This is mine — the second one, technically, because the first one lived on a WordPress blog that I let expire in 2019 along with a domain name I still miss.

So: hello. I'm Vlad. I write software for a living and I spent the years before that doing marketing, which is why this site has a **Marketing** folder sitting next to **Applications** and why I don't think that's strange.

## What you'll find here

- **Applications** — things I've built. Some are products with users, some are tools I made for myself and then polished because I couldn't help it.
- **Marketing** — campaigns, content programs and launches I've run. Numbers where I have them, honesty where I don't.
- **Blog** — you are here. Expect posts about building things, getting people to use them, and the overlap between the two.

## Why the desktop?

I wanted a personal site that felt like *a place* rather than a scrolling page, and I've always loved the clarity of early desktop interfaces: everything is a thing you can open, move and close. Folders are the navigation. Windows are the pages. There's a Terminal for people who'd rather type.

I wrote about how it's built in [Why my portfolio is a fake operating system](#/blog/why-my-portfolio-is-an-operating-system).
